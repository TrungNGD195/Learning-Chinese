# Panda illustration cast

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The final direction follows the
user's request to match the cartoon drawing of Bo, Trúc and Bút: large round
heads, small bodies, lively eyes, short furry paws and expressive acting.
The male panda wears jade and an ochre satchel; the female panda wears cinnabar
and a jade scarf with a bamboo ear ornament. Bo, Trúc and Bút retain their
existing identities. Human figures are replaced in the active illustration set.

The two-row sheets use 3×2 square-cell registration; the extra-word strip uses
3×1. Objects, numbers and the horse remain distinct from character depictions.
The scene atlas retains native alpha and opaque white paint on characters and
objects. Each PNG embeds its final prompt and has a provenance JSON sidecar.
The rejected realistic panda variants remain outside the project and are not
used by the application.
